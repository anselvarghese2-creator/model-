What We Offer

End-to-end landscape services

From concept sketches to ongoing care, we handle every phase of your outdoor space.

01

Landscape Design

Custom garden plans tailored to your property, lifestyle, and local environment — delivered with detailed drawings and plant schedules.

02

Garden Installation

Full installation services — planting, hardscaping, irrigation, lighting — executed by our expert ground crew on time and on budget.

03

Lawn Care

Regular mowing, edging, fertilisation, aeration and pest management to keep your lawn lush and healthy throughout the seasons.

04

Seasonal Planting

Rotating colour schemes with annuals and perennials timed to bloom in sequence, giving your garden year-round visual appeal.

05

Tree & Shrub Care

Pruning, shaping, disease treatment and structural support for mature trees and ornamental shrubs on residential and commercial properties.

06

Irrigation Systems

Smart drip and sprinkler systems designed for efficiency, saving water while ensuring every plant gets precisely what it needs.

Our Story

Rooted in craft, grown through trust

Verdant was founded in 2006 with a single belief: that beautiful landscapes should be accessible, sustainable, and deeply personal. Our team of horticulturists, designers, and craftspeople bring together science and artistry in every project we touch.

  • Certified landscape architects on every project
  • Native and drought-tolerant plant specialists
  • Sustainable practices & eco-conscious materials
  • Fully insured and licensed in all states we serve
